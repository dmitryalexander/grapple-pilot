Adds Grapple as a fourth selectable pilot tactical alongside Cloak, Stim and Active Radar Pulse. Select Grapple in the normal pilot loadout and use the normal tactical key; tap again to release. Includes universal powersliding, grapple/slide audio, a dedicated Grapple icon and native recharge bar.

Defaults: 10-second recovery after a clamped grapple, 1 second after a miss, and a 2-second interval between extra slide impulses. Slide impulse is 392.16 with a 600 added-speed ceiling. Host settings are editable in `scripts/vscripts/tactical/settings.nut`.

**Experimental prototype:** a computer freeze has been reported and its cause is unresolved. Four menu choices and Grapple selection were observed in an isolated game; latest HUD/boost edits pass offline checks but have not completed gameplay acceptance. Multiplayer, lifecycle and controller regression testing remain pending. This release makes no stability guarantee.

Requires Titanfall 1 / R1Delta (developed against 2.14.14). All participating hosts/clients need this version. Install as an alternative to Powder/Cooldown; back up your profile and follow INSTALL.md. Internal addon folder remains `R1Delta.Tactical`. Before removal, change every saved custom Grapple loadout to a stock tactical while the addon is loaded.

Download `Grapple-Pilot-0.1.0-prototype.zip` and its SHA256 checksum below. No game executables, native DLLs, local launcher/clipboard scripts, profiles, logs, research or developer test-game copies are included. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt for adapted scripts and game-audio provenance.
